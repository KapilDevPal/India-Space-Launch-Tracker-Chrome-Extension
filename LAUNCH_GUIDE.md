# 🚀 How to Launch the ISRO Launch Tracker – India Space Missions

To launch and use this custom Chrome Extension on your browser, follow these simple steps:

## Step 1: Open Extension Settings in Chrome
1. Open your **Google Chrome** browser.
2. Click on the **three vertical dots** (⋮) in the top-right corner of the window.
3. Go to **Extensions** -> **Manage Extensions**.
   *(Alternatively, you can type `chrome://extensions/` directly into your URL bar and press Enter).*

## Step 2: Enable Developer Mode
1. In the top-right corner of the Extensions page, you will see a toggle switch labeled **Developer mode**.
2. **Turn on Developer mode** (the switch will turn blue).

## Step 3: Load the Extension
1. Once Developer mode is enabled, three new buttons will appear in the top-left corner.
2. Click on the **Load unpacked** button.
3. A file browser window will pop up. Navigate to the folder containing this extension:
   `Desktop/school/crome extention`
4. Select the folder and click **Open** (or "Select Folder").

## Step 4: Pin and Launch
1. The extension is now installed! You should see its card in your Extensions list.
2. To easily access it, click the **Puzzle piece icon** (🧩) in the top-right toolbar of Chrome.
3. Find **ISRO Launch Tracker – India Space Missions** in the dropdown list and click the **Pin icon** (📌) next to it.
4. **Click the new Rocket icon** in your toolbar to launch the dashboard and view the live countdowns!

---

*Note: Any time you make changes to the code (like the recent timer fix), you can simply go back to `chrome://extensions/` and click the circular **Refresh icon** (🔄) on the extension's card to instantly load your updates.*
